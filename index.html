Services

What we build for our clients

We deliver software projects on a fixed-scope or monthly basis. Every engagement starts with a paid discovery call and ends with a system your team owns and runs.

Custom AI agents

Autonomous assistants trained on your data, your tone and your rules. They qualify leads, draft replies, summarise documents and take actions in your tools — with a human in the loop where it matters.

  • Knowledge base & document Q&A
  • Tool & API access for real actions
  • Guardrails, logging and handover to humans

Intelligent chatbots

Customer-facing assistants for your website, support inbox or messaging channels. They answer in your customers' language, 24/7, and escalate cleanly to your team when a human is needed.

  • Website widget & multi-channel support
  • Multilingual answers from your own content
  • CRM and helpdesk integration

WhatsApp Business API automation

Official WhatsApp Business Platform integrations: automated conversations, order updates, appointment reminders, notifications and payment follow-ups — on the channel your customers already use.

  • Cloud API setup & message templates
  • Automated flows with AI replies
  • Shared team inbox & analytics

Business process automation

We remove the manual steps between your systems: data entry, quotes, invoicing, reporting, onboarding. Custom integrations between your CRM, spreadsheets, email, payments and internal tools.

  • Workflow design & automation build
  • API integrations and data pipelines
  • Monitoring, maintenance & support

How we work

From first call to running system

  1. 01

    Discovery

    We map the process you want to automate, the tools involved and the outcome you expect. You get a written scope, a fixed price and a timeline.

  2. 02

    Build

    We develop and test the solution in short cycles, with a working demo you can try at each milestone. No black boxes.

  3. 03

    Launch & support

About

A UK technology company focused on applied AI

BONZINILABS LTD is a software company registered in England & Wales, based in London. We design and develop AI-powered tools and automation solutions for other businesses.

Our clients are small and mid-sized companies that want to serve customers faster without growing headcount: agencies, e-commerce brands, service providers and local operators. We work remotely with clients across Europe and Africa, and we deliver in English and French.

We are deliberately practical. We do not sell strategy decks — we ship working software, integrated with the systems you already run, and we stay responsible for it after launch.
